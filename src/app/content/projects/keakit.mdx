# KeaKit

KeaKit is a web application that brings together item rentals and service hiring in a single place, letting users build personalized "kits" around what they actually need. The platform works both ways: people looking for something find it quickly and easily, while owners of unused items can turn them into extra income, all through a centralized, flexible and hassle-free process.

## Key Features

KeaKit offers a complete platform that integrates item rental and service hiring into a single experience:

- **User management**: registration, login, profile editing and a rating system.
- **Item listings**: users can upload, edit and manage items available for rent.
- **Custom kit creation**: flexible combination of products and services into a single package.
- **Dynamic price calculation**: real-time cost display based on the kit configuration.
- **Logistics management**: selecting dates, locations and delivery or pickup conditions.
- **Integrated payments**: secure hiring and payment directly through the platform.
- **Rental tracking**: monitoring the status of active orders.

## My Role

I collaborated within a multidisciplinary team of more than 20 people, working under Scrum and agile development practices. My contributions included market research and analysis of competing platforms, as well as ongoing development of the platform through the implementation of new features, refactoring of existing components, and bug fixing.

## Challenges

Being part of KeaKit's development involved several challenges tied to my specific role:

- Coordinating effectively within a big multidisciplinary team, following Scrum practices and keeping development aligned with the rest of the team.
- Conducting market research and analyzing competing platforms.
- Implementing new features and refactoring existing components without breaking functionality already in use across the platform.
- Diagnosing and resolving bugs across a codebase built by many contributors, which required understanding logic and structures I hadn't originally written.

## Lessons Learned

Working on KeaKit gave me experience contributing to a real-world-like platform within a large team, coordinating work with 20 other people under Scrum and agile development practices.

It also taught me how market research and competitor analysis directly shape product decisions.

Finally, contributing to an evolving codebase helped me build practical skills in implementing new features, refactoring existing components, and resolving bugs while keeping the platform stable for other users and developers.
